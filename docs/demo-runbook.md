# WelcomeRO three-minute demo

## Before presenting

- Use a supported desktop browser and a stable connection.
- Confirm the production URL, Firebase authorized domain, Gemini request, photo upload, Romanian speech, microphone permission, and map tiles.
- Keep one redacted Romanian sample form ready.
- Keep screenshots or a short recording of the AI and microphone flows as a fallback.
- Start with no saved profile, or use a private browser window.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

“Romanian administrative information is spread across institutions and often assumes that a newcomer already knows which procedure applies. WelcomeRO turns a person’s situation into the next action, the documents to prepare, and the words to use at the office.”

## 0:20–0:45 — Personalisation

Choose **Try the sample journey**. Explain that it is a fictional non-EU international student in Timișoara with accommodation but no completed address registration. Mention that real onboarding also distinguishes employment stages and protection stages so incompatible procedures are not mixed.

## 0:45–1:35 — Complete one task

On the dashboard:

1. Show the recommended next task.
2. Open its concise guide summary.
3. Tick two documents as ready.
4. Open the appointment kit.
5. Show the Romanian questions and printable preparation sheet.
6. Mark the task complete and show the next task appear.

## 1:35–2:20 — Real-world assistance

Use one memorable capability:

- Upload the redacted sample form and show field-by-field guidance, or
- Use “Talk at the counter” for a short exchange.

Explain that AI supports the workflow; it does not decide which legal claims are true. Administrative answers are grounded in WelcomeRO’s curated, source-linked data.

## 2:20–2:45 — Trust and human support

Show a guide’s claim-specific source links and the source monitor. Explain:

- automated checks detect link and content changes;
- changes create a review PR;
- procedure advice is never changed automatically;
- LOGS and official institutions remain available for case-specific help.

## 2:45–3:00 — Outcome

“WelcomeRO does not replace an institution. It helps students, workers, and people seeking protection arrive prepared, communicate clearly, and complete the next correct step with less stress.”

## Questions to prepare for

- Who reviewed the administrative content?
- Which user groups tested the journeys?
- How does the app protect uploaded documents and personal data?
- What works when AI or the network is unavailable?
- How are source changes reviewed?
- How will users discover WelcomeRO after the hackathon?
