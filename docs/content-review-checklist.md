## Evidence check

For every eligibility statement, mandatory document, deadline, fee, processing time, and office detail:

- [ ] The linked page is from the responsible authority or an appropriate specialist source.
- [ ] The page was opened and the relevant section was read, not inferred from a search result.
- [ ] The statement applies to the audience and county described by the guide.
- [ ] Numbers, dates, addresses, and document names match the source.
- [ ] Source notes describe any limitation precisely.
- [ ] `verified`, `recommended`, and `confirm` labels match the strength of the evidence.
- [ ] Conflicting sources were resolved or clearly recorded.
- [ ] The guide’s `lastVerified` date reflects this review.
